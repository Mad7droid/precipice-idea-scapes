# Precipice 0.1.20

## Phones and tablets

- The editor is fully usable on a phone. Below 640px the canvas fills the screen; a bottom
  bar holds Outline, Add, Ask, Undo and Redo; the outline, inspector, Scapi, add menu,
  Settings and Help open as bottom sheets; the top bar folds Find, Capture, Publish, Export
  and Settings into More.
- Touch: a selection bar (Edit, Connect, Ask, Duplicate, Delete) above the selected block;
  tap a selected block to open the inspector further; two-finger tap undoes; double-tap on
  empty canvas fits; press and hold on empty canvas adds a block there. Help lists the
  gestures on touch screens.
- Phones open a scape on its first blocks at a readable zoom.
- Tablets (640–1023px): panels slide over the canvas instead of narrowing it, one at a time.
- Published scapes open as a readable list on phones, with connections as jump links, a
  Canvas / List toggle on every screen, and native Share where supported. Embeds are unchanged.
- Installable to the home screen (web app manifest, icons, standalone display).
- Inspector text typed just before the tab is hidden or closed is committed before autosave.

## Design system

- New tokens: safe-area insets, `--hit-min` (44px on coarse pointers), `--sheet-radius`,
  `--scrim`, `--z-sheet`, `--vh-dynamic`. Inputs are 16px on touch screens to stop iOS focus
  zoom, the one sanctioned exception to the 13px default.
- `src/core/viewport.ts`: breakpoints, touch timings, sheet snaps and the gesture table.
- Tailwind variants: `compact:`, `medium:`, `expanded:`, `coarse:`, `hoverable:`.

## Compatibility

No data, permission, credential or publishing change. The service worker serves the manifest
and icons stale-while-revalidate. CSP is unchanged; the new manifest is same-origin. The public
viewer still reaches nothing that can edit or publish (`src/viewer/bundle.test.ts`).

## Verification

`pnpm verify` and `pnpm test:mcp` before release. Layouts and gestures were checked in
headless Chrome with touch emulation at 390×844, 820×1180 and 1280×800.

## Known verification limits

Not yet checked on a physical iPhone or Android device: on-screen keyboard behaviour in iOS
Safari, notch spacing and home-screen install were not exercised on hardware.
